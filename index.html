StudyNotes

Handwritten study notes and PDF annotation for Windows.

StudyNotes is a small private desktop app for taking notes while studying: write with a pen or graphics tablet, annotate large lecture PDFs, organise notebooks in folders and search your notes.

Google Drive sync

StudyNotes can optionally back up and sync your notebooks with your own Google Drive. You sign in with your Google account inside the app.

  • StudyNotes only gets access to the files it creates itself (Google permission drive.file). It cannot see or change any other files in your Drive.
  • Your notes are sent only between your computer and your own Google Drive. There is no StudyNotes server.
  • You can sign out at any time in the app, and remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
