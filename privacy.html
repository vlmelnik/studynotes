Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Summary

StudyNotes is a desktop app. Your notes stay on your computer and, if you turn on Google Drive sync, in your own Google Drive. The developer does not collect, receive or store any of your data.

Data stored on your computer

Notebooks, imported PDFs, pictures and settings are saved as files in a folder on your computer that you choose. They are never sent to the developer.

Google Drive sync (optional)

  • If you sign in with Google, StudyNotes requests only the permission https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. With it, the app can only access files it created itself in your Google Drive. It cannot see, read or change any other files.
  • The app reads your account's e-mail address and name from Google Drive only to show you which account is signed in.
  • Your notebook files are transferred directly between your computer and Google's servers. There is no StudyNotes server in between.
  • The sign-in token is stored encrypted on your computer (Windows data protection). It is deleted when you sign out.
  • Google user data is used only to provide the sync feature. It is not shared with anyone, not sold, not used for advertising and not used to train AI models.

StudyNotes' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

  • Sign out in StudyNotes (Settings → Google Drive sync → Sign out).
  • Remove StudyNotes' access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the StudyNotes files in your Google Drive and the library folder on your computer.
